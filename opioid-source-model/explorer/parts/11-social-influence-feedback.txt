Population
	~	people
	~	Total population in US ages 12 and older, using NSDUH's population count. \
		Population is used in the model to standardize the effect of social \
		influence relative to the size of the current 'at risk' population. We \
		limit to persons 12 and older because we assume children under 12 are not \
		at great risk of initiating opioid misuse. [Source: NSDUH]
