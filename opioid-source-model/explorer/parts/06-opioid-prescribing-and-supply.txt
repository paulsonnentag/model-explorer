		 + Sensitivity of Rx supply to patients receiving prescription
	 + Sensitivity of Rx supply to Rx per person + Sensitivity of Rx supply to days per prescription\
		))
	             * Patients receiving opioid prescription annual relative 
	                  ^ (4 * Sensitivity of Rx supply to patients receiving prescription\
		/ (Sensitivity of Rx supply to MME per day
	 + Sensitivity of Rx supply to patients receiving prescription + Sensitivity of Rx supply to Rx per person\
		 + Sensitivity of Rx supply to MME per day)) * Prescriptions per person relative
	 ^ (4 * Sensitivity of Rx supply to Rx per person / (Sensitivity of Rx supply to MME per day\
		 + Sensitivity of Rx supply to patients receiving prescription
	 + Sensitivity of Rx supply to Rx per person + Sensitivity of Rx supply to days per prescription\
		)) 
	* Average days per prescription relative
	             ^ (4 * Sensitivity of Rx supply to MME per day / (Sensitivity of Rx supply to MME per day\

		))

Sensitivity of Rx supply to MME per day  = 1
	~	dmnl [0,2]
	~	[ASM] Weight parameter determining the relative importance of prescription \
		strengths (MME/day) vs. number of people with opioid prescriptions in \
		affecting the supply of 'excess' Rx available for misuse. A value of 1 is \
		neutral, giving equal importance to size & number. A values less than 1 \
		indicates that diversion is driven more by the number of people with a \
		prescription, while a value greater than 1 indicates diversion is driven \
		more by the strength of presriptions.
