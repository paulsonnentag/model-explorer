Total heroin users  = 
        Nondisordered heroin use 
             + Rx OUD with PY heroin total 
             + HUD total
	~	people
	~

HUD total=
	HUD no MOUD 
	             + SUM ( ( 1
	                       - Remission fraction in Tx[TxT!] ) 
	                       * HUD by MOUD[TxT!] )
	~	person
	~	Total number of people with heroin use disorder, including those in and \
		out of treatment.

Nondisordered heroin use  = 
        INTEG( Initiating heroin no Rx 
                  + Initiating heroin with Rx misuse 
                  - Developing HUD no Rx OUD 
                  - Net quitting heroin with Rx misuse 
                  - Net quitting NDHU 
                  - NonOD death NDHU 
                  - Overdose death NDHU , 
             Initial NDHU )
	~	people [?,300000]
	~	Individuals with Non-Disordered Heroin Use

Rx OUD with PY heroin total=
	Rx OUD with PY heroin no MOUD 
	             + SUM ( ( 1
	                       - Remission fraction in Tx[TxT!] ) 
	                       * Rx OUD with heroin by MOUD[TxT!] )
	~	person
	~

Total overdose deaths base Rx  = 
        Overdose death MU 
             + Overdose death Rx misuse 
             + Total overdose deaths Rx OUD no H 
                  * OD death fraction base Rx OUD no H 
             + Total overdose deaths Rx OUD with H
	~	people/year
	~

Total overdose deaths synth base  = 
        Total overdose deaths Rx OUD no H 
             * OD death fraction synth baseline Rx OUD no H
	~	people/year
	~

Total Rx misuse initiation  = 
        Initiating Rx misuse diverted 
             + Initiating Rx misuse own Rx
	~	people/year
	~	Total number of people who initiate Rx opioid misuse, regardless of the \
		source of their opioids.
	~	:SUPPLEMENTARY

Initiating heroin no Rx  = 
        Initiating heroin no Rx net 
             * Heroin availability coeff initiating NDHU no Rx 
             * Perceived risk coeff initiating NDHU no Rx 
             * Social influence coeff initiating NDHU no Rx
	~	people/year
	~	Individuals initiating NDHU without first using Rx opioids, ie initiation \
		of heroin use directly. [Source: SAMHSA]

Total heroin initiation  = 
        Initiating heroin no Rx 
             + Initiating heroin with Rx misuse 
             + Initiating heroin with Rx OUD
	~	people/year [?,500000]
	~

Initiating heroin with Rx misuse=
	Rx misuse no PY heroin 
	             * Initiation rate heroin with Rx misuse effective
	~	person/Years
	~

Initiating heroin with Rx OUD=
	Rx OUD no PY heroin no MOUD 
	             * Initiation rate heroin with Rx OUD effective
	~	people/year
	~

Fraction of heroin initiation with Rx OUD  = 
        Initiating heroin with Rx OUD 
             / Total heroin initiation
	~	dmnl
	~	Fraction of heroin initiation occurring among people with an Rx OUD. This \
		is an output variable used to compare to NSDUH estimates.
	~	:SUPPLEMENTARY

Fraction of heroin initiation with Rx misuse  = 
        Initiating heroin with Rx misuse 
             / Total heroin initiation
	~	dmnl
	~	Fraction of heroin initiation occurring among people with past year Rx \
		opioid misuse. This is an output variable used to compare to NSDUH \
		estimates.
	~	:SUPPLEMENTARY

Fraction of heroin initiation no Rx  = 
        Initiating heroin no Rx 
             / Total heroin initiation
	~	dmnl
	~	Fraction of all heroin initiation that comes from individuals with no \
		prior misuse of Rx opioids, ie the fraction that initiated heroin use \
		directly.
	~	:SUPPLEMENTARY

Rx OUD no PY heroin total=
	Rx OUD no PY heroin no MOUD 
	             + SUM ( ( 1
	                       - Remission fraction in Tx[TxT!] ) 
	                       * Rx OUD no heroin by MOUD[TxT!] )
	~	person
	~	Individuals with Rx OUD but no past year heroin use.

Initiating Rx misuse diverted  = 
        Initiating Rx misuse diverted net 
             * Perceived risk coeff initiating Rx misuse diverted 
             * Rx availability coeff initiating Rx misuse 
             * Social influence coeff initiating Rx misuse
	~	people/year
	~

Initiating Rx misuse own Rx=
	Patients with current month opioid Rx 
	             * Initiation rate Rx misuse own Rx effective
	~	people/year
	~

Total overdose deaths synth excess  = 
        Overdose death NDHU 
             * OD death fraction synth NDHU 
             + Total overdose deaths HUD 
                  * OD death fraction synth HUD
	~	people/year
	~	Total synthetic-involved overdose deaths among heroin users, including \
		both those due to baseline levels of synthetic use / exposure and to \
		heightened synthetic exposure post-2013.

Overdose death Rx misuse  = 
        Rx misuse no PY heroin 
             * Overdose death rate Rx misuse
	~	people/year
	~

Overdose death MU  = 
        Patients with current month opioid Rx 
             * Overdose death rate base MU
	~	people/year
	~

Overdose death NDHU  = 
        Nondisordered heroin use 
             * Overdose death rate NDHU
	~	people/year
	~

Total overdose deaths HUD  = 
        Overdose death HUD 
             + SUM ( Overdose death HUD in MOUD Tx[TxT!] )
	~	people/year
	~

Total overdose deaths Rx OUD no H  = 
        Overdose death Rx OUD no H 
             + SUM ( Overdose death Rx OUD no H in Tx[TxT!] )
	~	people/year
	~

Total overdose deaths Rx OUD with H  = 
        Overdose death Rx OUD with H 
             + SUM ( Overdose death Rx OUD with H in Tx[TxT!] )
	~	people/year
	~

        INTEG( Initiating Rx misuse diverted 
                  + Initiating Rx misuse own Rx 
                  - Developing Rx OUD 
                  - Initiating heroin with Rx misuse 
                  - Net quitting Rx misuse 
                  + Net quitting heroin with Rx misuse 
                  - NonOD death misuse 
                  - Overdose death Rx misuse , 
             Initial Rx misuse no H )

	~

Rx OUD no heroin in remission= INTEG (
	 SUM ( Tx exit in remission Rx OUD no H[TxT!] ) 
	                  - NonOD death Rx OUD no H in remission 
	                  - Relapsing to Rx OUD no H 
	                  + Remitting Rx OUD no H no MOUD Tx
	 -Stabilizing remission Rx OUD no H,
		 
		             Initial Rx OUD no H in remission)
	~	people
	~

Rx OUD with heroin in remission= INTEG (
	 SUM ( Tx exit in remission Rx OUD with H[TxT!] ) 
	                  - NonOD death Rx OUD with H in remission 
	                  - Relapsing to Rx OUD with H 
	                  + Remitting Rx OUD with H no MOUD Tx
	                   - Stabilizing remission Rx OUD with H,
		 
		             Initial Rx OUD with H in remission)
	~	people
	~

	                  - Stabilizing remission HUD,
		 
		             Initial HUD in remission)
	~	people
	~	Individuals with HUD in remission.
