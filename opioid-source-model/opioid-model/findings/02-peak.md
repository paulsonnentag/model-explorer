Heroin and fentanyl deaths keep rising for a few years and then decline under optimistic, middle and pessimistic assumptions alike, which the authors attribute mainly to the risk response cutting the number of people who start.

> In all three scenarios, the trend eventually reverses and deaths start to decline as the prevalence of heroin/IMF use falls. Projections from other models … may miss the impending peak and decline.

## Runs

- **Trends continue**: Inputs keep their recent trends, slowing to 2032.
- **Optimistic** (future Pos): Less fentanyl, more naloxone and treatment, fewer prescriptions.
- **Pessimistic** (future Neg): More fentanyl, less naloxone and treatment, prescribing cuts weaken.

543,000 to 842,000 deaths in 2020–2032 in the optimistic and pessimistic scenarios.

## Caveat

The authors also tested the risk response directly (X8_lk_sens.tab in their results). Re-fitted without it, the model matches history worse (average error 15.9% against 12.7%) and projects 16% more deaths for 2020–2032. Switching it off in the fitted model without re-fitting gives +294% and an average error of 197%, which is why this page does not offer that switch.
